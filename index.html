Excel to Swish QR Generator

How to Generate Swish QR Codes

  1. Prepare an Excel file with at least one of the following columns: Swishnumber, Message, Amount, and Title (the title on the card).
  2. Select which fields should be editable in the QR Code.
  3. Choose your preferred colors for the card.
  4. Upload the file using the area below.
  5. Click "Convert" to generate QR codes.
  6. Download will automatically start when all QR codes are generated.
